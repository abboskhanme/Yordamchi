Download a video for the owner from a link (`url`: YouTube or another video site) and send it to him, made to fit 50 MB; `start_s` and `end_s` (seconds from the start) cut a part of it, and `as_file` true sends it as a file. Use it only when he asks to download or send him a video. The result only says the download has started (`status`, `site`); the video, or the reason it could not come with what can be done instead, reaches him later through the bot, so say in one short line that it is on its way. To say what a video contains, use `analyze_video`.
